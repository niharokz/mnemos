# Welcome to Mnemos

This is the **demo user**. Everything here is a sample copy inside the app, so edit freely —
*Settings → Data → Reset demo* puts it back.

- Notes are plain files. Open the **Markdown tour** to see every feature.
- Tap ✏️ / 👁 at the top of a note to switch between editing and the live preview.
- `tables/expenses.csv` opens as a real table. Tap a cell to edit it.
- `logs/syncthing.log` is about 2 MB: it opens in the fast read-only viewer.
- `notes/todo.md` has a Syncthing **conflict** copy next to it. Open the copy to compare and resolve.
